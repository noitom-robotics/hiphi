Overview

HiPHI is a 617.5-hour optical MoCap benchmark for high-precision human motion and object-interaction. The dataset construction starts from lexical FrameNet seeds, expands them into Frame-LU motion units, and records synchronized human motion with object state when the motion requires it.

FrameNet / Frame-LU

HiPHI uses FrameNet frames and lexical units as motion seeds. Each Frame-LU expands through controlled realization factors such as speed, direction, posture, body-part involvement, and object/contact conditions.
